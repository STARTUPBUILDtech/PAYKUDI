PAYKUDI is a payment platform that lets you pay confidently for deals you'd normally be nervous about, at a distance you can't fully verify.

It runs on WhatsApp, so there's nothing new to download for the part that matters most: negotiating, paying, and confirming your deal. Payment stays protected until the buyer confirms they've received exactly what was ordered. And if the buyer goes quiet, it releases automatically after the allocated confirmation time, so sellers aren't left waiting forever either.

If any dispute arises, our AI reviews the case based on the evidence provided first, then a trained team member makes the final call.
