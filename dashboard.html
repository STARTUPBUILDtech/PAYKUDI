A: This order has a 01:59:59 inspection limit. If the counterparty does not fulfill requirements before it expires, Paykudi auto-resolves the order or processes payout.

Q: Can PayKudi cancel an order?

A: Paykudi holds the payment until delivery is confirmed by the buyer. Neither party can cancel unilaterally without mutual consent or a formal appeal review.

Q: Why is the inspection window 2 hours?

A: The 2-hour window gives the buyer sufficient time to unbox and verify item authenticity while protecting the seller against prolonged payout delays.
